\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\emph{Labels.} Label and flow-construction errors in NIDS benchmarks are well documented for CIC-IDS2017 and CSE-CIC-IDS2018~\cite{engelen2021troubleshooting,liu2022error}, and Flood et al.\ catalogue design smells of benchmark datasets~\cite{flood2024smells}. For CIC-IoT2023~\cite{neto2023ciciot2023}, Ramkumar et al.\ observed that the captures mix benign and attack traffic and re-filtered them per device by MAC address, using a device--attack mapping obtained from the dataset authors, but did not quantify the mixing~\cite{ramkumar2024diagnosing}. Vitorino et al.\ re-extract CIC-IoT2023 flows and assume that each capture contains only its named attack~\cite{vitorino2025revisiting}. IoTGeM reports benign packets inside the attack captures of another IoT dataset~\cite{kostas2024iotgem}. Background traffic in attack captures is therefore not new; we add a per-flow labelling from recorded evidence, the background share per capture, and the measured cost of file-name labels.

\emph{Splits.} Random splits of time-ordered data are a known pitfall in security machine learning~\cite{arp2022dos,pendlebury2019tesseract,apruzzese2023sok}, so a lower temporal score is expected. Temporal evaluations involving CIC-IoT2023 exist: Traffic-MoE uses a per-class chronological split on packet header and payload bytes, apparently including CIC-IoT2023~\cite{zhou2026trafficmoe}, and BRIDGE trains on earlier and tests on later traffic in a pooled benchmark that includes CIC-IoT-2023~\cite{bridge2026tchnet}. IoTGeM trains on telnet and tests on RTSP brute force, by design, and finds that the variants do not generalise~\cite{kostas2024iotgem}. Our split differs in being within each capture, on evidence-relabelled flows, and in tracing the gap to its cause and to boundary-spanning connections.

\emph{Live detection and exporters.} Learned detectors on IoT gateways are common~\cite{realguard2022}, as are Mininet/Open vSwitch~\cite{lantz2010mininet,pfaff2015ovs} prototypes that block flagged hosts and combine NFStream~\cite{aouini2022nfstream} with a classifier. Tang et al.\ separate benchmark evaluation from operational validation~\cite{tang2026hybrid}. Flow export and its timeouts are surveyed by Hofstede et al.~\cite{hofstede2014flow}; Pinto et al.\ and Vitorino et al.\ show offline that the exporter changes what a model sees~\cite{pinto2025hera,vitorino2025revisiting}. Late idle expiry in NFStream was reported before without a cause~\cite{nfstream120}. Our gateway is not new in its parts; we contribute the fidelity check and what it exposed.

\emph{Response.} Blocking via OpenFlow/OVS drop rules and gating a block on several verdicts or on confidence appear in SDN mitigation work~\cite{bawany2017ddos,yan2016sdn}; our ARP rule is the classic arpwatch idea~\cite{arpwatch}. Few works we found report false-block counts on replayed traffic.
